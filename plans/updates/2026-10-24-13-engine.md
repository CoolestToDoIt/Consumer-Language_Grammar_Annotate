Continuing on `feat/gloss-engine` after source-affix index commit `50beecf`.

## Recent work

Large candidate pools now use exact heap-based top-K selection rather than
sorting every rule. Small pools still sort, avoiding extra heap overhead on
online corrections. The complete ranking key and prediction semantics remain
unchanged. Isolated Tsez ranking over 77,448 hypotheses measured median 37 ms
versus 92 ms over five repetitions. Full fitting measured 0.809 s; treat that
end-to-end timing as a single-run diagnostic.

Full Lezgi/Tsez candidate outputs, reloads, and the entire online correction
trace match previous artifacts exactly. New tests cover both sides of the
strategy boundary, small/empty pools, and exhaustive ordered top-K equivalence.
Details: `reports/gloss/performance/bounded-ranking.md`.
