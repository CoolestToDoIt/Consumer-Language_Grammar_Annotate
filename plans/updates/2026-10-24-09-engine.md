## Implemented

`GlossLearner.fit(records) -> self` applies an iterable in order and prepares rankings once at the batch boundary. It appends to existing state, preserves duplicate/revision/version semantics, and leaves the successfully applied prefix inference-ready if input fails. Existing `correct()` still prepares the next prediction immediately. Version-1 checkpoint reconstruction uses bulk ingestion and restores its authoritative stored version.
