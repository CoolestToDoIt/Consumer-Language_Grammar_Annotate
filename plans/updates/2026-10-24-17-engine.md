# Worker A — exact corpus tier boundaries

## Recent work

The interlinear reader previously recognized any line starting with `\\t` or
`\\g`. A `\\translation` or `\\glossary` metadata tier could therefore be treated
as a source or target tier, or trigger a false duplicate-tier error. It now
requires the exact tag followed by whitespace or end of line. Space/tab
delimiters remain supported; other tiers remain discarded.

60 tests pass. Added exact-tag and tab-delimiter coverage, metadata preceding
and following valid input, and rejection when similarly named tags are the
only apparent source/target. Full Lezgi/Tsez aligned membership, complete
candidate artifacts in all modes, and checkpoint reconstruction are unchanged.
Reports: `reports/gloss/performance/{lezgi,tsez}-exact-tiers.json`.
